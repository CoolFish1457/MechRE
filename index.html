The Meaning Behind Jaga

ජාග

Jaga (ජාගරණය) — derived from the Sinhala word "Jāgarana" — means alertness, vigilance, and wakefulness. It embodies the essence of a guardian who never sleeps, constantly watchful and ready to respond.

"Always awake, always aware — the eternal guardian."

Our Purpose

Mission

Our mission is to empower visually impaired people with safer, smarter, and more independent mobility.

Through JAGA, we combine robotics and intelligent technology to create a future where navigating the world is more accessible to everyone.

Constant Vigilance

Like its name Jaga, our robot maintains 24/7 awareness, detecting obstacles and hazards in real-time.

App-Powered Control

Voice commands, destination input, and real-time audio guidance through your smartphone — intuitive and modern.

Protective Guardian

Jaga embodies the spirit of a watchful protector — always alert, ready to respond, and dedicated to your safety.

How Jaga Works

Three simple steps to independent navigation

1. Voice Command

Simply tell the app your destination. Jaga's AI processes your voice and plans the optimal route instantly.

2. Smart Navigation

Ultra-sonic sensors scan the environment in real-time while the onboard computer calculates safe paths.

3. Audio Guidance

Receive clear, concise voice instructions through your phone. No physical feedback needed — just pure audio guidance.
