The Meaning Behind Jaga

ජාග

Jaga (ජාගරණය) — derived from the Sinhala word "Jāgarana" — means alertness, vigilance, and wakefulness. It embodies the essence of a guardian who never sleeps, constantly watchful and ready to respond.

"Always awake, always aware — the eternal guardian."

Simple & Intuitive

How Jaga Works

Three simple steps to independent navigation

1. Voice Command

Simply tell the app your destination. Jaga's AI processes your voice and plans the optimal route instantly.

2. Smart Navigation

Ultra-sonic sensors scan the environment in real-time while the onboard computer calculates safe paths.

3. Audio Guidance

Receive clear, concise voice instructions through your phone. No physical feedback needed — just pure audio guidance.

Our Mission

To create Jaga — an affordable, culturally-rooted robotic guide dog that restores independence and mobility for the visually impaired community, powered entirely by a smartphone app.

Constant Vigilance

WRO 2026 Champions

We're proud to be the World Robotics Competition (WRO) 2026 Senior Category Champions at Buhari Serendib School. Jaga was judged on innovation, technical execution, and social impact.

  • App-controlled navigation: Voice destination setting & real-time guidance
  • Obstacle course: Dynamic avoidance using ultra-sonic sensors
